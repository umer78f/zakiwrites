Case 02 / Cold email

WalaPlus.
Employee-retention email campaign

A three-email outbound sequence that reframes employee retention as a business priority: lead with turnover, make the cost tangible, then open a conversation.

Client
WalaPlus
Scope
Cold email strategy and copy
Sequence
3 emails
Audience
CEOs and COOs at 200+ employee companies
Market
Saudi Arabia
Primary action
Book a short meeting

The message arc

Three emails to make
retention a priority

The sequence moves through three related pressures rather than repeating one pitch: the shock of losing people, the operational cost of absence, and the competitive disadvantage of trained employees leaving.

Each email closes with a simple invitation to talk, keeping the next step consistent while the reason to care changes.
