The strategy

Porsche MEA’s
social media strategy

Porsche's regional audience doesn't just buy a car — they buy into a legacy. The messaging framework balanced two registers: the authoritative, passionate voice the brand demands, and a witty, relatable tone that earns traction on social.

Owned carousels carried the brand's premium essence — performance, design, and belief — while curated UGC reels tapped into internet culture (the 🐐, green flags, POV formats) to make an aspirational brand feel personal. Every caption was written to stop the scroll first and sell the dream second.
